# inculva — Company Overview

## What is inculva?

inculva is a web accessibility platform that helps websites achieve WCAG compliance automatically. Site owners add a single `<script>` tag to their website, and their visitors get an accessibility widget with 24 features — no code changes or page rebuilds required.

## Mission

Over 1 billion people worldwide live with some form of disability. The vast majority of websites remain inaccessible, creating barriers to information, services, and opportunities. inculva was founded to change that. We use technology to automatically detect, fix, and monitor accessibility issues, making compliance effortless and inclusive design the default.

Our goal is simple: a world where every website works for every person.

## Core Values

- **Inclusion First** — We design for the widest possible range of users. Accessibility is our foundation, not an afterthought.
- **Innovation** — We push the boundaries of what technology can do for accessibility, continuously improving our scanning and remediation tools.
- **Transparency** — We are transparent about what our platform does, how we use data, and what compliance means.

## What We Offer

1. **Accessibility Widget** — A drop-in overlay that gives visitors 24 accessibility features across vision, reading, motor, and calm categories (text resizing, high contrast, dyslexia font, color-blind mode, screen reader, and more).
2. **WCAG Compliance Scanning** — Automated scanning engine powered by axe-core that crawls your pages and reports violations with severity ratings and fix guidance.
3. **Analytics Dashboard** — Track widget feature usage, session data, and visitor interaction patterns.
4. **Knowledge Base** — Comprehensive bilingual (EN/TR) knowledge base covering all WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 success criteria with fix examples.
5. **Accessibility Statement Generator** — Generate EAA Article 13 compliant accessibility statements directly from the dashboard.

## How It Works

1. **Install the Widget** — Add a single `<script>` tag to your website. No dependencies, no build step required.
2. **Configure & Customize** — Choose which accessibility features to enable, set your language, and customize the widget to match your brand.
3. **Scan & Monitor** — Run automated WCAG scans and track your compliance score over time.
