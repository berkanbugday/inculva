## Billing FAQ

**Can I switch plans at any time?**
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. All paid plans come with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

**What happens if I exceed my pageview limit?**
We will notify you when you approach your limit. Your widget will continue to work, but we may ask you to upgrade if high traffic persists across billing cycles.

**Do you offer yearly billing?**
Yes, yearly billing is available with approximately a 20% discount.

Contact us at hi@inculva.com for enterprise pricing tailored to your organization's needs.
